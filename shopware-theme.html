      Ask a Question
      How long does it take to build a custom Magento 2 store?
      A standard Magento 2 implementation usually takes between 2 to 4 months. More complex enterprise solutions with extensive customizations and integrations may take 4 to 6 months or more, depending on your specific requirements.
      Can you migrate my existing store to Magento 2?
      Yes! We provide seamless migration services from platforms like Magento 1, Shopify, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce to Magento 2, ensuring that all your products, customer data, and SEO equity are preserved.
      Do you provide custom module development?
      Absolutely. While there are thousands of ready-made extensions, we frequently develop bespoke modules to address unique business logic, complex shipping rules, or specific third-party integrations that off-the-shelf plugins can't handle.
      Is Magento 2 suitable for B2B eCommerce?
      Magento 2 (especially Adobe Commerce) is widely considered one of the best platforms for B2B. It natively supports features like company accounts, customized catalogs, quoting systems, requisition lists, and advanced pricing models out-of-the-box.
