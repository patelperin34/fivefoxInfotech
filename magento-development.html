      FAQ

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Have a question about Magento development? We'd love to help.

      Ask a Question
      How long does it take to build a Magento 2 store?
      A standard Magento 2 store typically takes 6–12 weeks depending on complexity. Simple stores with minimal customization can be ready in 4–6 weeks, while enterprise stores with custom integrations and extensions may take 3–6 months.
      Should I choose Magento Open Source or Adobe Commerce?
      Magento Open Source is ideal for small to mid-size stores. Adobe Commerce (Enterprise) is recommended for large-scale stores needing advanced B2B features, Cloud hosting, and dedicated Adobe support. We can guide you to the right choice based on your business needs and budget.
      Can you migrate my existing store to Magento 2?
      Yes. We handle migrations from Magento 1, WooCommerce, Shopify, PrestaShop, and other platforms to Magento 2. All your products, customers, orders, and SEO URLs are migrated safely without data loss.
      Do you build custom Magento 2 extensions?
      Absolutely. We build fully custom extensions to meet your specific business requirements. All extensions are built following Magento coding standards and are upgrade-safe, meaning they won't break when you upgrade your Magento version.
      What is Hyvä and do you develop Hyvä themes?
      Hyvä is a modern, performance-focused frontend theme for Magento 2 that replaces the heavy Luma theme. It dramatically improves Core Web Vitals and page speed. Yes, we build custom Hyvä themes and our team includes certified Hyvä developers.
      Do you provide post-launch support?
      Yes. Every project includes 30 days of free post-launch support. After that, we offer flexible maintenance packages to keep your store secure, updated, and running at peak performance.
