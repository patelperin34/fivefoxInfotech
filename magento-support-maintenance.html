      Ask a Question
      What is included in Magento 2 maintenance?
      Our Magento 2 maintenance covers security patches, performance optimization, bug fixing, extension updates, database optimization, server monitoring, backup management, and regular health audits — all handled by certified Magento experts.
      How quickly do you respond to critical issues?
      For critical issues (store down, payment failures), our response time is under 1 hour. High priority issues are addressed within 4 hours, and standard requests are handled within 1 business day.
      Do you support both Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce?
      Yes, we support both Magento 2 Open Source (Community Edition) and Adobe Commerce (Enterprise Edition), as well as Adobe Commerce Cloud deployments.
      Can I upgrade my plan later?
      Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your support plan at any time. We'll pro-rate any billing adjustments accordingly and transition smoothly with no service interruption.
      Will you work with my existing hosting provider?
      Yes, we work with all major hosting providers including AWS, DigitalOcean, Cloudways, Nexcess, Magento Cloud, and dedicated servers. We adapt to your existing infrastructure.
      Is there a minimum contract period?
      We offer both monthly and annual plans. Annual plans come with a discount. There's no long-term lock-in — you can cancel with 30 days notice on our monthly plans.
