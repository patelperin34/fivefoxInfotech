      Why Upgrade Now

      Risks of Running an Outdated Magento

      Every day on an old version puts your store at risk. Here's what you're missing — and what you're exposed to.

      Security Vulnerabilities

      Older versions receive no security patches, leaving your store open to exploits, malware, and data breaches.

      Poor Performance

      Newer Magento versions bring significant speed improvements — faster page loads mean higher conversions.

      No Vendor Support

      Adobe ends support for older versions, meaning no bug fixes, no patches, and no compatibility updates.

      Extension Conflicts

      New extensions and integrations are built for the latest Magento — outdated stores lose access to the best tools.

      SEO Disadvantage

      Core Web Vitals and site speed improvements in newer versions directly impact your search engine rankings.

      PCI Compliance Risk

      Outdated platforms may fail PCI compliance checks, putting your ability to accept card payments at risk.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Have more questions about upgrading your Magento store? We're happy to help.

      Ask a Question
      How long does a Magento 2 upgrade take?
      The timeline depends on your store's complexity. A standard upgrade (2.3.x to 2.4.x) typically takes 3–7 business days. Stores with many custom extensions or heavy customizations may take 2–3 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline after the pre-upgrade audit.
      Will my store go down during the upgrade?
      No. We perform the entire upgrade on a staging environment. The live store is only switched over once everything is fully tested and approved. The actual cutover typically takes 15–30 minutes and is scheduled during off-peak hours.
      Will I lose my orders, customers, or product data?
      Absolutely not. Data preservation is our top priority. We take full backups before any work begins and validate every piece of data — orders, customers, products, CMS pages, and configurations — after the upgrade.
      What if my extensions don't support the new version?
      We identify incompatible extensions in the pre-upgrade audit. For each one, we either find a compatible replacement, upgrade it ourselves, or redevelop the functionality natively. You'll know the full scope before we start.
      Do you support upgrades to Adobe Commerce (Enterprise)?
      Yes. We handle upgrades for both Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce (Enterprise Edition), including Adobe Commerce Cloud migrations. Our team is experienced with both editions.
      What support do you provide after the upgrade?
      All upgrades include 30 days of free post-launch monitoring and bug fixing. If any issues arise from the upgrade during this period, we fix them at no additional cost. We also offer ongoing maintenance plans for continued support.
